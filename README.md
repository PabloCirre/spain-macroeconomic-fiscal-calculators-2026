# Spain Macroeconomic, Fiscal & Corporate Law Engines (2026)

Open-source deterministic calculation engines (Python 3) and verified primary datasets (CSV / JSON) for Spanish macroeconomic analysis, Treasury Bill auctions (*Letras del Tesoro*), personal income tax (*IRPF*), corporate insolvency (*TRLC*), minority shareholder rights (*Art. 348 bis LSC*), and B2B commercial late-payment claims (*Law 3/2004*).

 Maintained by the Data & Research Desk of **[Diario Económico](https://diarioeconomico.net/)** ([Estudios Económicos](https://diarioeconomico.net/estudios/) · [16 Simuladores Interactivos](https://diarioeconomico.net/calculadoras/) · [Consulta Jurídico-Mercantil](https://diarioeconomico.net/consulta-abogado/)).
